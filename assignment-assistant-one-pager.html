A grading tool for teachers

Assignment Assistant

Turns a folder of digital submissions into one stack of papers you grade in one pass — instead of clicking through the LMS one student at a time.

What it's for

Normally you grade digital work inside the LMS's built-in submission editor — click into a student, wait for the viewer to load, mark it up, enter the score, click back out, find the next student. It's clunky, it's slow, and you spend more time navigating it than reading student work.

Assignment Assistant compiles every student's submission into one PDF instead. You grade the whole class as a single document, top to bottom, the way you'd work through a stack of papers on your desk — and the built-in editor drops out of the process entirely.

How you use it — start to finish
  1. 1

    Pick your class and the file you downloaded

    Choose the section, then the submission ZIP sitting in Downloads. Nothing to set up, nothing to rename.

  2. 2

    It assembles the stack for you

    Unzips everything, keeps only each student's newest attempt, converts Word files to PDF, merges students who uploaded several files, and matches every submission back to your roster by name.

  3. 3

    One combined PDF opens, ready to grade

    The whole class in last-name order, every page stamped with the student's name and “page 2 of 3.” Grade it on screen, mark it up on a tablet, or print it like a paper stack.

  4. 4

    Enter every grade on one screen

    The full roster at once — a grade box beside each name and a comment box if you want one. Badges mark who didn't submit, whose file wouldn't open, and whose page count doesn't match the rest of the class.

  5. 5

    Grades move to the gradebook

    Typed once, in one pass, then synced — not re-entered student by student inside the LMS.

Also included

Completion mode

Grading for “did you do it?” — one click gives the same points to everyone who turned it in, and lists everyone who didn't.

Attach a rubric page

Automatically add the same rubric or feedback sheet before or after every student's submission.

Split & hand back

Once you've marked up the combined PDF, it splits back into per-student files and re-zips them for return.

Email who's missing

Email the whole class, or only the students who never submitted this assignment.

Running term statistics

A per-student tally of missed and late work across the semester, with room for your own notes.

Plain activity log

A readable record of exactly what happened to this assignment — useful when a student disputes it.

One pass, not thirty

A 30-student assignment becomes one PDF and one grade screen instead of thirty trips through the LMS grader.

Nothing disappears quietly

Missing, unreadable, and unmatched submissions come back as a list before you grade — not as a blank cell in week twelve.

Every paper looks the same

Same order, same page size, same name stamp — so you're comparing student work instead of fighting file formats.

To be clear

It doesn't grade for you. Every score and every comment is still your judgment — the tool only removes the clerical work around it: the downloading, unzipping, sorting, name-matching, page-stamping, and re-typing.
